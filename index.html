Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your device. You can play on a plane, in a tunnel, or with the phone in airplane mode.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no profile. Your progress is stored on your phone.

How does multiplayer work?

Multiplayer is played on one device. Players take turns and pass the phone between them — there's no online matchmaking.

Where did my saved progress go?

Progress lives on the device, so deleting the app removes it. It isn't backed up to a server, and it doesn't transfer between phones.

Can I turn off sound or vibration?

Yes — both are switches in Settings inside the app.

Privacy

The game has no accounts and no ads, and it doesn't collect anything that identifies you.
