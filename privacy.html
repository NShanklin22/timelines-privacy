Privacy Policy

Timelines: Line the Times

Last updated 29 July 2026

Short version: the game has no accounts, no ads, and no trackers. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, or contacts, and it never sends your gameplay anywhere. The only thing that leaves your device is an automatic crash report when the app fails, and those reports can't identify you.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember where you left off:

  • Your best score and streak
  • Your sound and vibration preferences
  • An in-progress game, so you can resume it later

This information stays on your iPhone. It isn't uploaded, synced, or shared, and no one — including me — can see it. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends an automatic diagnostic report so the problem can be found and fixed. These reports are handled by Sentry, a crash-reporting service acting as a processor on my behalf.

A crash report contains technical details about the failure — the error itself, where in the code it happened, the app version, your device model, and your iOS version. It does not contain your name, email address, contacts, location, or any of your game data, and it isn't linked to your identity or used to track you across apps or websites.

Crash reports are used for one purpose: fixing bugs. Sentry's own handling of the data it processes is described in its privacy policy.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking
  • No access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, because none is collected

Apple's role

The game is distributed through the App Store. If you download it, rate it, or use the iOS share sheet, Apple handles that interaction under Apple's own privacy policy. I don't receive personal information from Apple about who you are.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. There's nothing to sign up for and no way to communicate with other players through the app.

Your rights

Because no personal information is collected or stored on any server, there's nothing held about you to access, correct, or delete. Data saved on your device is removed by deleting the app. If you have a question about any of this, write to me at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
